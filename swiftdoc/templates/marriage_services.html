Certificate of No Impediment

Issued by the Registrar of Marriages in Kenya confirming that a Kenyan citizen has no existing marriage record and is legally free to marry abroad. It is often required by foreign authorities before they can register a marriage involving a Kenyan national.

REQUIREMENTS

These are the details we need from you for this service

  • Certified copy of Passport
  • Certified copy of Birth Certificate
  • Proof of marital status (such as an affidavit or statutory declaration)
  • Mandatory 10-day public notice
  • Details of intended marriage abroad
  • Copy of Passport(bio-data page)
  • Two-passport size photos
