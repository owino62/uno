Work Permits

Select your work permit class

Class A

Prospecting/Mining

Issued to individuals engaging in prospecting for minerals or mining activities, allowing them to legally operate within Kenya’s mining sector.

Class B

Agriculture

Granted to those investing in or managing agricultural ventures such as farming, livestock, or horticulture.

Class C

Prescribed Professions

Designed for professionals such as doctors, lawyers, engineers, and other regulated fields, enabling them to practice their profession in Kenya.

Class D

Employment

For individuals offered employment by a specific employer in Kenya, covering most formal job placements.

Class F

Manufacturing

Issued to investors or managers establishing or running manufacturing industries in Kenya.

Class G

Trade/Business

For entrepreneurs and business owners intending to engage in trade or establish commercial enterprises.

Class I

Religious/Charitable Activities

Granted to missionaries, clergy, or individuals involved in charitable or humanitarian work.

Class K

Residency without Employment

For persons wishing to reside in Kenya without engaging in employment, often retirees or dependents.

Class M

Refugees

Issued to recognized refugees, allowing them to legally reside and engage in permitted activities within Kenya.
